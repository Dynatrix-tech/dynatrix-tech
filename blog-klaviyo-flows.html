The 4 Klaviyo flows worth building before any campaign

Skip the campaign calendar until these four automations are live — flows outperform one-off sends for most stores.

LF
Leo Fischer
Social & Content Lead · 7 min read

Clients often come to us wanting a campaign calendar first — a plan for weekly promotional emails. We almost always redirect that energy toward flows first, and here's why: flows run automatically, forever, without you writing a single new email once they're built.

1. Welcome series

Not one email — a short series, spaced over several days, that introduces the brand, sets expectations, and moves someone toward their first purchase. This is often the single highest-converting flow in the account, because it's reaching people at their most engaged moment.

2. Abandoned cart

The classic, for good reason. A well-timed, well-written abandoned cart sequence recovers real revenue that would otherwise just disappear. The key is timing and tone — a reminder, not a guilt trip.

Flows run automatically, forever, without you writing a single new email once they're built.

3. Post-purchase

The most underused flow we see. This is where you set expectations on shipping, ask for a review at the right moment, and start building toward a second purchase — all without asking for anything upfront.

4. Win-back

Targets people who used to buy and have gone quiet. A good win-back flow costs nothing to run and consistently recovers a slice of revenue you'd otherwise write off entirely.

Why this order

These four flows run in the background permanently, compounding in value the longer they're live. A campaign calendar is valuable too — but it's work you have to keep doing. Flows are work you do once. Build the foundation first.

What "done" actually looks like

A flow isn't finished the day it goes live. We revisit each one roughly every quarter — checking open rates, click rates, and revenue per recipient — and rewrite the weakest-performing email in the sequence rather than leaving a mediocre flow running untouched for a year. Small, regular refinements consistently outperform a "set it and forget it" approach.

Key takeaways

  • Build welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase, and win-back flows before investing in a campaign calendar.
  • Flows compound in value over time since they run automatically once built.
  • Revisit and refine flows quarterly — don't treat them as permanently finished.
