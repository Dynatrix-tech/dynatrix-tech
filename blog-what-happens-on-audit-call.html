What actually happens on an audit call

No pitch deck, no pressure — here's exactly what we do in the 30 minutes.

BE
Benjamin Emmanuel
Founder, Dynatrix Tech · 5 min read

A lot of people hesitate to book a call because they've been burned before — "free audit" that turns out to be a thinly disguised sales pitch. So here's exactly what happens, no surprises.

Before the call

We ask for your store or site link ahead of time, so we're not spending your 30 minutes clicking around live. We do a real look beforehand — speed, structure, obvious conversion leaks — so the call starts with findings, not small talk.

During the call

We walk through what we found, in plain language, and rank it by what actually matters — not a generic checklist, but what's specifically true for your store. You'll leave with real, specific things to fix, whether or not you ever hire us. That's not generosity for its own sake — it's the fastest way for you to tell if we actually know what we're talking about.

You'll leave with real, specific things to fix, whether or not you ever hire us.

What we don't do

No slide deck. No countdown-timer discount that expires the moment the call ends. No vague "we'll send a proposal" without specifics. If it makes sense to work together, we'll say so plainly, and talk real numbers. If it doesn't, we'll tell you that too.

After the call

You get a short written summary of what we covered — so you're not relying on memory, and you have something concrete to act on either way.

Why I run it this way

I spent six years freelancing before starting Dynatrix Tech, and I sat on the other side of enough "free strategy calls" that were really just 25 minutes of sales pitch and 5 minutes of substance. I decided early on that our calls would be the opposite ratio. If someone walks away with three things they can genuinely fix themselves and never books a second call, that's a completely fine outcome. Most people come back anyway — but it has to be because the first conversation was actually useful, not because they felt cornered into it.

Key takeaways

  • We review your store before the call, so the time is spent on findings, not exploration.
  • You get specific, actionable fixes regardless of whether you hire us afterward.
  • No countdown discounts, no pressure tactics — a written summary follows the call either way.
