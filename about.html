/ Our story

Small on purpose.

Dynatrix Tech started in 2017 as two freelancers trading Shopify builds and SEO retainers. Nine years on, we're still a tight team — because that's the only way build and growth stay this connected.

/ Our mission

One team. Every discipline. Zero handoffs.

Most agencies grow by adding departments — a build team here, an SEO team there, a social team that's never met either. Every handoff between them is a chance for your project to lose momentum, context, or accountability. Our mission is to close those gaps entirely: one senior team, across every discipline your growth depends on, working from a single shared view of what's actually happening in your store or site.

That's why we keep our client roster deliberately small. It's the only way the people who scope your project are still the ones building, running, and reporting on it a year in — not a rotating cast of account managers.
