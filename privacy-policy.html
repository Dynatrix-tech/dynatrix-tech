Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit this site or work with us.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who we are

Dynatrix Tech ("we," "us," "our") is a Shopify and Webflow build-and-growth studio. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website (dynatrixtech.com) and in the course of providing our services.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Contact details submitted through our contact form, "Get Started" form, or audit request popup — such as your name, email address, company name, website, and project details.
  • Email address, if you subscribe to our blog or newsletter updates.
  • Any information you share with us directly, such as by email or during a consultation call.

We also automatically collect limited technical information when you browse our site — such as your browser type, device type, pages visited, and approximate location — typically through standard analytics tools.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to inquiries and follow up on project requests
  • To schedule and prepare for audit calls or consultations
  • To send occasional updates, offers, or content, if you've opted in
  • To improve this website and understand how visitors use it
  • To meet legal, accounting, or security obligations

4. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with trusted third-party tools we use to run our business — such as email delivery, scheduling, and analytics providers — solely to the extent needed for them to perform their function. We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Cookies and tracking

This site may use cookies or similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand site usage. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

6. Data retention

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law — for example, to maintain records of client engagements.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, or to withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how we handle your information, reach out at hello@dynatrixtech.com.
