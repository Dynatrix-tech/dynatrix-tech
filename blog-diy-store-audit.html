How to audit your own store in 15 minutes

Before you book a professional audit, here's a quick self-check that catches the most common issues.

PN
Priya Nandakumar
SEO Strategist · 7 min read

You don't need us to tell you if something's obviously broken. Here's a fast, honest self-check you can run before deciding whether a deeper audit is worth it.

1. Load your homepage on your phone, on real data (not wifi)

Turn off wifi and load your site on regular mobile data. If it takes more than three or four seconds to feel usable, that's costing you visitors right now — most people won't wait longer than that before bouncing.

2. Try to buy something from your own store

Actually go through checkout, on mobile, as if you were a real customer. Count the taps. Anything over four or five steps from product page to order confirmation is worth questioning.

If it takes more than three or four seconds to feel usable, that's costing you visitors right now.

3. Search your own brand name in an incognito window

What shows up? If a competitor's ad appears above your own listing, or your Google Business Profile is missing or outdated, that's an easy, high-impact fix.

4. Check your last three email campaigns' open rates

Under 20% open rate on a warm list usually means either your subject lines need work or your list needs cleaning — both fixable in an afternoon.

5. Look at your top product page like a stranger would

Is the price clear? Is there a real reason to trust the brand — reviews, guarantees, real photos? Missing trust signals are one of the most common, most fixable conversion leaks we find.

6. Check what happens on a 404

Type a random, made-up page into your own site's URL bar. If you land on a generic, unbranded error page with no way back, that's a small but real leak — anyone who mistypes a link or clicks an old bookmark just bounces straight off your site instead of finding their way back in.

What this doesn't replace

This 15-minute version catches the obvious stuff. It won't catch crawl errors, structured data issues, or app bloat slowing down your backend — that's what a full technical audit is for. But if even one of the six checks above turns something up, it's worth having a real conversation before it compounds.

Key takeaways

  • Test your own checkout and homepage speed on real mobile data, not wifi.
  • Search your own brand name incognito to catch competitor ads or an outdated Business Profile.
  • A missing or generic 404 page is a small, easy-to-fix leak worth checking.
