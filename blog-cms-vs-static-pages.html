CMS collections vs. static pages: when to use each

A practical framework for deciding what belongs in Webflow's CMS.

By Marcus Whitfield · 5 min read

One of the most common questions we get early in a Webflow build is simple: "should this be a CMS collection, or just a regular page?" Get this decision wrong early and it's expensive to unwind later — so here's the framework we actually use.

Use a CMS collection when...

  • You'll have more than 3–5 of something, and that number will keep growing (blog posts, case studies, team members, locations)
  • The content follows a repeatable structure — same fields, different values, every time
  • Someone on your team who isn't a developer will need to add or edit entries regularly

Use a static page when...

  • The page is genuinely one-of-one — your homepage, your pricing page, your contact page
  • The layout needs real design flexibility that doesn't fit a repeatable template
  • It changes rarely enough that editing the page directly isn't a burden

The mistake we see most often

Teams either over-CMS everything (turning a 3-page "Our Values" section into a bloated collection nobody needed) or under-CMS the wrong thing (hard-coding a "Team" page that needs a developer every time someone new joins). The fix is almost always to ask: will a non-developer need to touch this in the next six months? If yes, it belongs in the CMS. If no, keep it simple.
